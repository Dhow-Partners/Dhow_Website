Why we started Dhow Partners.

We kept seeing the same thing. Good companies arrived in Kenya and lost months between lawyers, accountants, banks and regulators, with nobody running the whole process. We started Dhow Partners to be that person: one contact who brings in the right firms and keeps the work moving until you are up and running.

The name.

For centuries, dhows carried trade between East Africa and the rest of the world. We do the same job for modern business: getting you to where you want to be.

Any sector. One process.

Whether you grow coffee, build software or import machinery, setting up follows the same steps: registration, licences, banking, hiring and launch. We bring in the specialists who know your industry.
