1. Who we are

This privacy policy explains how Dhow Partners Ltd ("Dhow Partners", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you visit our website, contact us, book a call or work with us. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. This policy is issued under the Data Protection Act, 2019 (Laws of Kenya) and the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, and it should be read together with our Terms and agreement.

2. Scope

This policy covers personal data collected through this website, our booking and contact forms, our membership application and reminder forms, email, phone, WhatsApp, events and meetings, and in the course of providing our services. It does not cover the websites or practices of the independent firms we introduce, or of any third-party platform you reach through a link on our website. Each of those parties is responsible for its own processing and its own privacy notice.

3. Personal data we collect

We collect only what we need. This may include: identity details (your name and job title); contact details (email address, phone or WhatsApp number); organisation details (company name, country, sector); enquiry details (what you need help with and anything you choose to tell us); booking details (the date and time you select); correspondence and meeting notes; documents you or your company send us so that the licensed partner firm doing the work can use them, such as certificates of incorporation, company records (CR12), copies of directors' and shareholders' IDs or passports, KRA PINs, tax compliance certificates, permits and licences, contracts, and financial and bank statements; and technical data (IP address, device and browser type, pages visited and the time of your visit). Some services need sensitive personal data as defined in section 2 of the Act: company registration and bank account opening can involve property details such as shareholding and financial records, and work permit and dependant pass applications can involve family details such as a spouse and children. We collect these only when the service you ask for requires them, only to pass them to the licensed firm doing that work, and for no other purpose. We do not ask for, and ask you not to send us, other sensitive personal data, such as health, biometric, ethnic or religious data.

4. How we collect it

We collect personal data directly from you when you complete a form, book a call, email or message us, or meet us. We collect technical data automatically when you use the website. We may receive limited business contact data from people who refer you to us, or from public sources such as company registers, where the law allows.

5. Membership applications

When you apply for Dhow Partners Membership at dhowpartners.com/apply, we collect your name, role, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, company name, whether the business is registered, its sector, years trading, annual revenue range (optional), website (optional), the plan you choose, how you heard about us, what you need help with and what you want to achieve in the next twelve months. We use these details to assess your application, to contact you about it and, if you are accepted, to set up and run your membership, including the event representation and investor introductions you ask for. We process them on the basis of your consent, which you give on the form, and to take steps at your request before you become a member. Your application is stored in Google Sheets, a service provided by Google that we use to manage applications. Applications that do not lead to membership are kept for up to twenty-four months, in the same way as other enquiries, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

6. Investor introductions

The membership application form has an optional box that allows us to share your business profile with vetted investors. We share your profile only if you tick that box, and even then we ask you before every introduction. You can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to us, and we will stop sharing your profile from then on.

7. Reminder sign-ups

Before applications open, you can ask us to remind you when they do. For this we collect only your name and email address, store them in Google Sheets and use them to send you one reminder email from our Google account. We do not use them for anything else, and we delete the reminder list after the application round closes.

8. Lawful basis for processing

We process personal data only where section 30 of the Act allows it. Depending on the purpose, we rely on: your consent; the need to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, or to perform a contract with you; compliance with a legal obligation that applies to us; and our legitimate interests in responding to enquiries, running and improving our business and keeping our systems secure, where those interests are not overridden by your rights. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before it.

9. How we use personal data

We use personal data to: respond to your enquiry and confirm your booking; prepare for and hold our calls and meetings; prepare a written plan or proposal at your request; introduce you to independent firms, with your agreement; provide, manage and invoice our services; keep records required by law; protect our website, systems and business against fraud, misuse and security threats; and, where you have agreed, send you occasional updates that you can opt out of at any time. We will not use your personal data for a new purpose that is incompatible with these purposes without telling you first and, where required, obtaining your consent.

10. Sharing personal data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We share it only where necessary and only with: independent licensed firms we introduce to you, and only after you have agreed to the introduction; vetted investors, only if you agreed to this on the membership application form and agree to each introduction; service providers who host our website, email, booking calendar, forms and membership records (including Google, which provides the spreadsheet where applications are stored), and who process data on our instructions under written terms that require them to keep it secure and confidential; professional advisers bound by confidentiality; and public authorities, courts or regulators where the law requires it. Any firm you contract with directly becomes responsible for its own processing of your data.

11. Transfers outside Kenya

Some of our service providers may store or process data outside Kenya. We transfer personal data out of Kenya only in line with sections 48 to 50 of the Act, that is, where there are appropriate safeguards for the security and protection of the data, where the destination offers an adequate level of protection, or where another condition under the Act is met. Where required, we will seek your consent before transferring sensitive personal data out of Kenya.

12. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected for, in line with section 39 of the Act. As a guide, enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are kept for up to twenty-four months; client and engagement records are kept for as long as the engagement lasts and then for the period required by tax, accounting and other laws of Kenya; and technical logs are kept for a short period for security purposes. When data is no longer needed, we delete it, anonymise it or securely destroy it.

13. How we protect personal data

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration and destruction, as required by section 41 of the Act. These include encrypted connections, access limited to people who need it, strong authentication and confidentiality obligations on everyone who handles your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to review our measures regularly.

14. Data breaches

If a breach of security leads to a real risk of harm to you, we will notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within seventy-two hours of becoming aware of it, as required by section 43 of the Act, and we will tell you without undue delay where the law requires it, together with the steps we are taking and any steps you can take to protect yourself.

15. Your rights

Under section 26 and related provisions of the Act you have the right to: be informed of how your personal data is used; access the personal data we hold about you; object to all or part of the processing; have false or misleading data corrected; have data that is no longer needed, or that was processed unlawfully, deleted; restrict processing in certain circumstances; receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and have it moved to another controller where technically possible; withdraw consent at any time; and not be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing that significantly affects you. We do not carry out such automated decision-making.

16. How to exercise your rights

To make a request, contact us through the details on our Contact page and mark your message "Data protection request". We may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request, so that we do not release data to the wrong person. We will respond within the timelines set by the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021. We do not charge a fee unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive, in which case we may charge a reasonable fee or decline it and explain why.

17. Direct marketing

We will only send you marketing messages where you have agreed to receive them or where the law otherwise allows it. Every message will tell you how to opt out, and opting out is free and takes effect promptly.

18. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to operate the website and keep it secure. With your consent, we also use Google Analytics cookies to understand how the website is used, which pages are visited and whether booking and contact links work, so we can improve it. Analytics only runs after you select Accept all in the cookie banner. If you select Necessary only, we use only the cookies and storage the website needs to work. IP addresses are anonymised and we do not use advertising cookies. We also keep two small notes in your browser: your cookie choice, and whether you have closed our membership notice during your visit, so we do not show it again. These are necessary and are not used to track you. You can change your choice at any time through Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. You can refuse or delete non-essential cookies at any time through your browser settings, and the website will continue to work.

19. Children

Our website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of eighteen. If we learn that we have done so without valid parental or guardian consent as required by section 33 of the Act, we will delete it.

20. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to other websites and platforms, including our booking calendar and social media pages. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their privacy notices before you give them any personal data.

21. Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your personal data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

22. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in the law or in how we work. The current version will always be on this page with its effective date. Where a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to your attention.

23. Effective date

This privacy policy is effective from 4 November 2026.
