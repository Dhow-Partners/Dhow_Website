Common questions.

Can you register my company in Kenya?+

Yes. Our own team registers your company with the Business Registration Service (BRS), gets your KRA PIN and applies for your business permits. You have one named contact from start to finish.

Can a foreign company or investor set up a business in Kenya with you?+

Yes. We work with foreign companies and investors setting up in Kenya. Your written plan sets out the steps for your business, including any extra licences your sector needs.

What do your partner firms do?+

Licensed law firms handle contracts, licences, work permits and data protection. Certified accountants handle bookkeeping, VAT, payroll tax and audits. Vetted firms handle bank accounts and investor documents, recruitment and payroll, marketing and launch, and your website and systems. Each firm contracts with you directly, and we coordinate them.

How long does it take, and what does it cost?+

It depends on your business and the permits you need. After the free 30-minute call, you get a written plan within 48 hours: the steps, the order, who does what and the cost.

Is the first call free?+

Yes. The first 30-minute call is free. Members of Dhow Partners Membership also get free advice calls on a need basis.

Where is your office?+

519 Embassy House, Nairobi, Kenya. We work with companies setting up in Kenya and across East Africa.
