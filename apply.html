Questions and answers

Can't find your answer? Write to hello@dhowpartners.com or WhatsApp +254 716 688 988.

Who can apply?

Any business operating in Kenya, and founders who are about to register one. You should be the founder, a director or a manager of the business.

Are spaces limited?

Yes. Spaces in round one are limited, so make sure you apply before the deadline.

When is the deadline?

Wednesday 13 January 2027. We make all our decisions after the deadline, and every applicant hears from us by Friday 15 January.

How do you choose members?

Our team reads every application. We look at what your business needs, the stage it is at, and whether our membership can genuinely help you.

When will I hear back?

By Friday 15 January 2027. Every applicant gets a decision by email. If you are accepted, your acceptance email is followed by a welcome call.

How long does membership last?

One year. The programme officially starts on Monday 18 January 2027 and runs for a full year.

What is free?

Your first 30-minute call with us is free for anyone, and applying for membership is free. Members also get free advice calls on a need basis, whenever they want a follow-up. Everything else is covered by your plan, or quoted before any work starts.

When do I pay?

Only after you are accepted. Nothing is charged when you apply. Once we accept you, we send you a payment link.

Can I cancel, and can I get a refund?

Membership fees are not refundable. You can cancel at any time by writing to hello@dhowpartners.com, and your membership stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Yearly plans include two months free: KSh 10,000 a year for Starter and KSh 50,000 a year for Growth.

Will my price go up later?

Not if you join in round one. You keep your round one rate for as long as you stay a member.

Can you represent my business at events?

Yes. Every member can ask us to represent their business at major events, at a discounted member rate. Growth members get priority.

Will you share my details with investors?

Only if you tick the investor box on the form, and even then we ask you before every introduction. We never share your details without your consent.

Is Dhow Partners a law or accounting firm?

No. Our own team handles company setup: registration, KRA PIN and business permits. Legal, tax, accounting and other specialist work is done by independent, licensed partner firms that contract with you directly, and we coordinate them.
